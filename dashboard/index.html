    How the model works

    One function scores every protocol from the snapshot’s own figures. No protocol name is special-cased and no row is hand-picked.

    Implied value = round(TVL × ratio), where ratio is the median mcap/TVL of the protocols in the same category that already trade.

    Ratio rule. 5+ comparables: the category median (HIGH confidence). 2–4: the category median shrunk halfway to the global median (MEDIUM). 0–1: the global median (LOW). The ratio is clipped to [0.01, 1.00]. The global median across 128 trading protocols is 0.2138.

    Launch score P = 0.35 × size + 0.25 × growth + 0.20 × maturity + 0.20 × reach. Size is log TVL over the largest watchlist TVL ($2,754M); growth is the 7-day change winsorised to [−20%, +20%]; maturity peaks at 90–730 days with no token and falls after that; reach is chain count capped at 10. P runs 0.11–0.85.

    Score (expected value) = implied value × P, using the unrounded P. Rank follows implied value, because the board’s rank 1 must be its most valuable protocol; score_rank is the analyst’s ordering by score.

    What this data cannot tell us

    • No points programs, airdrop hints, docs, socials, funding rounds, team, governance forum or tokenomics draft. A protocol could have a points program live and this snapshot would look identical.
    • No user counts, volume, revenue or fees. TVL can be one whale; a $50M single-depositor vault and a $50M thousand-user DEX look the same here.
    • No FDV or supply, so no airdrop share is knowable from this file — and implied value is an implied market cap, not FDV.
    • No time series beyond the 7-day change: no 30/90-day trend, and no way to tell a spike (one protocol is up 4,448% in a week) from a trend.
    • Nothing after the snapshot date, 2026-11-14. Any launch, points program or TVL move after that is invisible.

    Coverage: who is on the board, and who is not

    The board carries 40 watchlist protocols plus 138 early rows below the $50M floor — 178 rows in all. The Live view sets aside the protocols that already trade, showing the top 24 by market cap with their real values, never mixed into the ranking.

    Left out by rule, not by judgement: protocols that already record a token; names ending in a version marker (V2, v3, 1.2, …); the Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC and CEX categories; names containing a whole word wrapped, staked or staking; and names beginning with an exchange name (Binance, Coinbase, Bybit, Kraken, Bitget, OKX, Bitfinex, Gemini). Those rules reproduce the watchlist’s own cleanliness test, minus the floor — so the early radar is every clean pre-token protocol below $50M, with no TVL cut.
